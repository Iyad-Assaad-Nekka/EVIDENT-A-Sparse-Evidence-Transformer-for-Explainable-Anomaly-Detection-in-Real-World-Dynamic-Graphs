\subsection{Injected Benchmark}
\label{sec:injected}

Table~\ref{tab:injected} places EVIDENT within the injected-anomaly
benchmark on UCI-Messages. EVIDENT-S attains $0.9766 \pm 0.0050$ AUC,
ahead of every learned detector in the table and of the parameter-free
degree heuristic at $0.8112$, while consuming twenty per cent of its
evidence pool. The comparison is deliberately unfavourable to EVIDENT:
fixed-cardinality resampling removes the cardinality channel described in
Section~\ref{sec:leak}, which the remaining entries retain.

An ungated model trained at full density, with the rationale objectives
disabled so that it is in-distribution on the complete pool, reaches
$0.9881 \pm 0.0008$ AUC, $0.7892 \pm 0.0166$ AP and $0.8720 \pm 0.0325$
P@100. Retention under the bottleneck is therefore $0.988$ on AUC but only
$0.851$ on both AP and P@100. A restriction that costs slightly over one
per cent of AUC removes a seventh of the precision at the head of the
ranking. The divergence is a second instance, internal to a single method,
of the metric disagreement documented across the benchmark, and it
reinforces the case for reporting P@100 alongside AUC rather than in place
of it.

The density-matched ladder in Table~\ref{tab:uci_ladder} is correctly
ordered throughout, and the empty mask sits at exactly $0.5000$: with all
tokens removed every score is identical, confirming that removal is
complete. The separations are nonetheless small. Ranking
($\mathrm{top}_k - \mathrm{bottom}_k$) gives $+0.0349 \pm 0.0197$
($1.77\sigma$), necessity against a density-matched disjoint subset gives
$+0.0122 \pm 0.0068$ ($1.79\sigma$), and the comparison against an
unrestricted random subset gives $+0.0056 \pm 0.0054$ ($1.03\sigma$). None
reaches the $2\sigma$ threshold met on Bitcoin-OTC.

We attribute this to token exchangeability rather than to any failure of
the gate, and Table~\ref{tab:uci_ladder} supplies the mechanism directly:
the model's \emph{lowest}-ranked twenty per cent of tokens still attains
$0.9417$. On a benchmark this close to saturation, and with tokens carrying
only a role class and an elapsed time, almost any admissible subset is
sufficient evidence, leaving the selector little to order. UCI-Messages is
an unsigned interaction log with no ratings and no edge attributes, so the
heterogeneous token features that lift the Bitcoin necessity gap to
$+0.2145$ ($6.6\sigma$) cannot be constructed on it. The corpus therefore
instantiates precisely the near-exchangeable regime in which the
preliminary Bitcoin design measured $+0.0006$ ($0.04\sigma$).

\paragraph{Token heterogeneity governs explainability}
In a preliminary design where tokens carried only a role class and a
timestamp, tokens were near-exchangeable and the necessity gap was
indistinguishable from zero ($+0.0006$, $0.04\sigma$) even though the
architecture was identical. Enriching token features raised it to
$+0.2145$ ($6.6\sigma$). The same pattern recurs across corpora rather than
across designs: on UCI-Messages, where the data admit no richer tokens, the
identical architecture yields $+0.0056$ ($1.03\sigma$) while detecting at
$0.9766$ AUC. Detection strength and rationale strength are thus separately
governed, and a model may be simultaneously accurate and unable to
distinguish its evidence. Whether a rationale can be meaningful is a
property of the representation, not only of the selection mechanism---a
consideration absent from the post-hoc literature, which measures
attribution quality without asking whether the underlying tokens are
distinguishable at all.

On the injected benchmark (Section~\ref{sec:injected}) EVIDENT detects
strongly but its rationale does not separate: no density-matched
comparison reaches $2\sigma$, and the lowest-ranked subset already attains
$0.9417$ AUC. We report the injected results as evidence of the
representational bound on explainability rather than as an explainability
result. Those figures are averaged over four seeds rather than five, a
fifth run having been lost before its results were persisted; the
remaining seeds are tightly concentrated ($\pm 0.0050$ AUC) and a further
seed would not alter the conclusion.
